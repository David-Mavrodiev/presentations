Fractional property ownership via KYC‑gated NFTs

One‑click access to income‑producing property • Automated dividend distribution • Regulated secondary transfers

For discussion with prospective partners & investors. Not investment or legal advice.

“We combine the transparency and programmability of blockchain with the stability and income potential of real estate—making diversified property investing simple, compliant, and liquid.”

Problem

  • High minimums & complex paperwork block everyday investors from quality real estate.
  • Cross‑border ownership, tax, and compliance are burdensome; property is illiquid.
  • Traditional SPV participation is opaque and slow; dividends & reporting are manual.

Opportunity

  • Tokenized real‑world assets (RWA) enable programmable ownership & compliance controls.
  • Security‑aware token standards + KYC‑gated transfers unlock a compliant secondary market.
  • Investors seek yield, transparency, and liquidity—especially in income property.

Solution — How It Works

  1. KYC/Wallet Whitelist: Investors onboard via AML/KYC; only verified wallets can hold tokens.
  2. Invest: Fund property pools in fiat or stablecoins; choose Auto‑Allocation or Manual selection.
  3. Acquire: Regional entity/SPV acquires the property once target is reached (escrowed flows).
  4. Mint: NFTs represent fractional economic rights in the SPV (dividends, exit proceeds).
  5. Operate: Rent collected; net income distributed to token holders in fiat or stablecoins.
  6. Trade: KYC‑gated secondary transfers; later list on licensed venues (ATS/MTF) where applicable.
  7. Exit: On sale, SPV distributes profits; NFTs are redeemed/burned.

MVP: issuer‑approved private transfers between whitelisted wallets; phase‑2: licensed secondary market.

Legal Foundations

  • Company law: SPVs per property; articles/operating agreements bind on‑chain to off‑chain cap table.
  • Securities law: NFTs represent investment rights → treat as securities; use exemptions or prospectus where needed.
  • AML/KYC: Verify investors, sanction‑screen wallets, Travel Rule records for large transfers.

Jurisdiction‑agnostic baseline; local specifics applied per market (UK/EU/US notes later).

What the NFT Legally Represents

Equity token

  • Represents shares/units of SPV.
  • Strong claim; transfers more constrained.

Trust / Beneficial interest

  • Token = rights under trust that owns SPV shares.
  • Flexible; requires trustee/registrar.

Debt token (notes)

  • Loan to SPV with interest & security.
  • Simpler transfers; less upside.

Pick one per asset; reflect in corporate docs & smart‑contract controls.

Distribution Waterfall

  • Payouts in fiat or stablecoins to verified recipients; full audit trail.

Secondary Trading & Exit

  • Phase 1 (MVP): Private transfers among KYC wallets with issuer/TA approval.
  • Phase 2: Resale on licensed venues (US: ATS; EU/UK: MTF) with whitelist checks.
  • Exit: On sale/refi, distribute proceeds; redeem/burn NFTs.

Jurisdiction Quick Notes

UK

  • FCA financial promotions; MLR registration for AML.
  • Consider custody/client‑money rules; AIFMD if fund‑like.

EU

  • Security tokens → MiFID II/Prospectus; check exemptions.
  • AIF assesment for pooled vehicles; MiCA scope (non‑securities).

US

  • Reg D 506(c) (accredited) + Reg S (offshore); Rule 144 lockups.
  • Secondary via ATS or issuer‑approved private transfers.
