Problem

Imagine an everyday investor, someone with savings set aside and a desire to grow wealth through alternative investments—real estate, infrastructure, private equity, and other high-return assets. They quickly discover that the doors to quality deals are closed to them. The buy-in levels are set at an exclusive tier, the paperwork is endless, and the entire process feels built for institutions—not individuals.

Even if they try to look abroad, the maze only deepens: every country has its own rules, taxes, and compliance hurdles. Cross-border ownership becomes a nightmare, and once invested, they realize their money is locked up for years. Alternative investments are among the least liquid asset classes, making it almost impossible to exit when cash is needed for opportunities or emergencies.

Those who do manage to invest through traditional SPVs, funds, or direct ownership find the experience opaque. They wait months for updates, dividends are distributed manually, and reports arrive late and unclear. What should feel like ownership feels instead like being shut out from real control and transparency.

Solution — How It Works

First, every investor completes KYC/AML verification. Their wallet is whitelisted, ensuring only approved participants can hold or trade our tokens.

Next, they can invest in asset pools using either fiat or stablecoins. Some will prefer our Auto-Allocation mode, where funds are spread across diversified projects automatically, while others may opt for Manual selection to choose specific assets themselves.

Once the target amount for a project is reached, our regional SPV acquires the asset, with all funds securely managed through escrow until milestones are met.

At that point, NFTs are minted. These tokens represent each investor's fractional economic rights — income distributions, resale proceeds, and exit profits across all asset classes.

The asset then enters the operate phase: income is generated (rent, dividends, interest, etc.) and, after expenses, net income is distributed to token holders directly in fiat or stablecoins.

For flexibility, investors can also trade their NFTs. At MVP stage, this means private transfers between verified wallets. Later, we will integrate with licensed secondary markets (ATS/MTF), unlocking full liquidity.

Finally, at exit — when the asset is sold or reaches maturity — the SPV distributes profits back to investors, the NFTs are redeemed and burned, and the cycle is complete.

How We Structure Investments

Each asset — whether real estate, infrastructure, private equity, or commodities — is held by a local Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV), a standard legal company (Ltd/LLC) that owns the asset. Investors never deal with complex paperwork or legal structures — their rights are represented by NFTs linked to that SPV.

The SPVs are controlled by regional subsidiaries, all owned by our Global Holding Company. This ensures compliance in each market while keeping everything coordinated under one roof.

Investor money flows through escrow accounts. Funds are released to asset managers, developers, or sellers only after milestones are verified, giving investors security at every step across all asset classes.

In simple terms: Investor → Escrow → SPV → Asset (Property/Infrastructure/Private Equity/etc.). NFTs mirror that ownership and profits flow back as dividends or sale proceeds.

How It Works — End to End

  1. Onboarding: Investor signs up, passes KYC/AML, wallet is whitelisted.
  2. Investing: They fund an asset pool (fiat or stablecoins) — auto-allocated or chosen manually across different asset classes.
  3. Acquisition: Once the pool is full, the SPV acquires the asset (property, infrastructure, private equity stake, etc.) through escrow.
  4. NFT Issuance: Investors receive NFTs proving fractional rights in the SPV.
  5. Operations: Income generated (rent, dividends, interest, etc.) → profits automatically distributed to NFT holders.
  6. Liquidity: NFTs can be resold — initially between verified wallets, later on licensed secondary markets.
  7. Exit: On asset sale or maturity, SPV distributes profits, NFTs are redeemed and burned.

A complete alternative investment cycle — digital, transparent, and liquid across all asset classes.

Compliance Built-In

Because NFTs represent real financial rights, compliance is at the heart of the platform:

  • KYC/AML: Every investor verified by providers like Sumsub, Onfido, or Veriff. Wallet is whitelisted only after approval.
  • Smart Contracts: Standards like ERC-1155 block transfers to unverified wallets, ensuring only compliant trading.
  • Legal Documents: Each SPV’s articles explicitly recognize NFT holders as the economic owners, securing rights to income and profits.
  • Funds Protection: Escrow, milestone releases, and trustee oversight ensure capital cannot be misused.

In short: off-chain KYC verifies the person → on-chain whitelist enforces it → SPV legal docs bind it all together.

The Foundation

Our platform is built on proven technology — carefully chosen for security, scale, and compliance.

At its core, we use an EVM blockchain, with Polygon as the target network for low fees and strong ecosystem support. On top of that, we write smart contracts in Solidity, using standards like ERC-1155 to handle fractional ownership and enforce investor rights.

The application itself looks and feels like a modern fintech product: a React frontend for investors, powered by a NestJS backend for business logic. Infrastructure is automated with Terraform (CDKTF), ensuring deployments are consistent, scalable, and secure.

Built into the core platform are AI-powered risk assessment and automated valuation engines. We use Python-based machine learning models for real-time risk scoring, integrated with Bloomberg and Refinitiv data feeds. Asset valuations leverage automated valuation models (AVM) and DCF analysis, with Chainlink oracles providing tamper-proof market data.

In short: blockchain where ownership matters, web technologies where user experience matters, AI where risk and valuation matter.

Proof of Concept (Day-0)

Our initial build already demonstrates the full investor lifecycle with integrated risk and valuation capabilities. From day one, users will be able to:

  • For acquired assets: View real-time risk scores (1-10 scale) and automated valuations, then mint fractional tokens at listing, purchase them, and transfer compliantly.
  • For assets in funding: Contribute to an investment pool smart contract with live risk monitoring. If funding succeeds, tokens are minted automatically; if not, contributions are refunded.
  • For income distribution: Income collected by the SPV is distributed directly and automatically to NFT holders, in fiat or stablecoins, based on their share.
  • For ongoing monitoring: Real-time risk updates, automated revaluations, and stress testing results displayed in the investor dashboard.

This POC proves our ability to mint, trade, and distribute income from day zero — with professional-grade risk assessment and valuation built-in from the start, completing the investment cycle in a digital, transparent, and compliant way.

Business Model

Meet our platform's revenue story: When Sarah invests in a solar farm project, we earn a small platform fee on the capital raised. This covers the behind-the-scenes essentials like due diligence, legal structuring, and deploying the smart contracts that make the investment secure.

As the solar farm begins generating income, we receive a modest management fee tied to the assets under administration. This ensures we can handle ongoing operations — from compliance checks to investor reporting — without ever taking money out of Sarah's returns.

When Sarah later decides to sell her NFT to another investor, we facilitate the trade and collect a transaction fee — just like an exchange would, but for alternative investments.

As we grow, we'll introduce premium services such as advanced analytics, institutional API access, and white-label solutions for other platforms. The more assets we tokenize, the more opportunities we create for sustainable revenue.

Simple model: we succeed when investors succeed. The better the projects perform and the more trading activity we facilitate, the stronger our business becomes.
